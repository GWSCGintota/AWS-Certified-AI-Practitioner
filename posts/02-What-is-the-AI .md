# What is AI (Artificial Intelligence)?

**AI (Artificial Intelligence)** is a branch of computer science that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- **Learning:** Learning patterns from data.
- **Reasoning:** Using information to solve problems.
- **Decision-making:** Making predictions or selecting actions.
- **Understanding language:** Understanding and generating human language.
- **Recognizing images:** Identifying objects, faces, and patterns in images.
- **Automation:** Performing tasks with minimal human intervention.

## Real-world Examples of AI

| Application | How AI is Used |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Understands questions and generates responses |
| Netflix | Recommends movies based on viewing preferences |
| Google Maps | Predicts traffic and suggests routes |
| Face Recognition | Identifies or verifies faces |
| Self-driving Cars | Detects roads, vehicles, and pedestrians |
| Amazon | Recommends products to customers |

## How Does AI Work?

A typical AI system works through the following process:

### 1. Data
- Collect images, text, numbers, or other information.
- Data is used to train AI models.

### 2. AI Model Training
- Algorithms learn patterns from data.
- The model identifies relationships and patterns.

### 3. Trained AI Model
- The trained model processes new information.
- It uses learned patterns to interpret inputs.

### 4. Prediction or Output
- The model generates answers, recommendations, predictions, or decisions.

### AI Workflow

Data
↓
AI Model Training
↓
Trained AI Model
↓
Prediction or Output

## AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning

These concepts are closely related, but they are not the same.

### 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

- The broad field of building intelligent machines.
- Enables computers to perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence.
- Includes machine learning, deep learning, and rule-based AI systems.

### 2. Machine Learning (ML)

- A subset of Artificial Intelligence.
- Allows machines to learn patterns from data.
- Reduces the need to explicitly program every decision rule.

**Example:**
An email spam detection system learns from previous emails to classify new emails as spam or not spam.

### 3. Deep Learning (DL)

- A subset of Machine Learning.
- Uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers.
- Can learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

**Example:**
A facial recognition system uses deep learning to recognize or verify faces in images.

### Relationship Between AI, ML, and DL

Artificial Intelligence (AI)
└── Machine Learning (ML)
└── Deep Learning (DL)

**Key Difference:**

- **AI:** Makes machines capable of intelligent tasks.
- **ML:** Enables machines to learn patterns from data.
- **DL:** Uses multilayer neural networks to learn complex patterns.

## Summary

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** enables computers to perform intelligent tasks.
- **Machine Learning (ML)** allows computers to learn from data.
- **Deep Learning (DL)** uses multilayer neural networks to learn complex patterns.
- **AWS AI Services** provide cloud-based tools to develop and deploy AI applications without always building models from scratch.
